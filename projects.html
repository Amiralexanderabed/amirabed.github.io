BiosphereLive

An interactive globe of life on Earth. Spin it and explore 846 land ecoregions and 269 marine regions — each with its biome, Köppen climate classification, and the species that live there.

Built the whole pipeline: Python scripts that fetch and rasterize the source geodata, and a dependency-free WebGL2 renderer that draws the result at interactive frame rates.

Deep TimeLive

A scroll-driven history of Earth across 4.6 billion years. Continents drift continuously on a globe reconstructed from the Müller plate rotation model, alongside 23 chapters from the Hadean to now.

Fossil localities come from the Paleobiology Database and are clickable at every period. Plate rotations are validated against the GPlates Web Service to a median agreement of 0.000035°.

ENLIGHTEN — occupancy-aware light switches

Automated home light switches that turn on when someone sits down near the corresponding light. Occupancy is detected with a TensorFlow vision model; the switches talk over BLE.

A retrofit design — it drives existing switches rather than requiring rewiring.
